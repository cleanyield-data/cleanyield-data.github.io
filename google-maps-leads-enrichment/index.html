Enrich Google Maps leads with emails and phones

Short answer: Paste your Google Maps export into Clean Yield’s Company Enrichment as it is: it reads each business’s own website and adds its emails, phone and social profiles next to your row. It does not scrape Google Maps, so you need the list first.

Already exported businesses from Google Maps? Paste the rows as they are (JSON, a CSV or Google Sheet link, or a dataset ID) and get each business's email, phone in E.164 and social profiles, read from its own website. Your original row comes back next to the contacts. This does not scrape Google Maps.

For agencies and sales teams that prospect local businesses (restaurants, clinics, salons, home services) and have a Google Maps list with no emails.

Try the Lite version first (no per-result charge) Full version in the Apify Store

How to use it

In the Apify Store (no code)

  1. Start with the Lite version (up to 25 results per run, no per-result charge), or open the full version, and click Try for free (Apify account, free plan included).
  2. Paste your list in the form, or use the input above.
  3. Click Start and download the results as JSON, CSV or Excel.

From your code (API)

curl -X POST "https://api.apify.com/v2/acts/cleanyield~company-enrichment-from-website/run-sync-get-dataset-items?maxTotalChargeUsd=1" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $APIFY_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
  "rows": [
    {
      "title": "Versailles Restaurant",

Create an API token in Apify Console → Settings → Integrations. maxTotalChargeUsd caps what the run can cost; the run stops there.

n8n and Make

In n8n, add the official Apify node; in Make, use the Apify app. Choose the Actor cleanyield/company-enrichment-from-website, paste the input JSON above, and read the dataset items in the next step. You can also schedule the Actor in Apify and send the results to a webhook, Google Sheets or Slack.
